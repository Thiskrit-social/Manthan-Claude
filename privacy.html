Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. Gather is an early (beta) service.

Gather helps people plan events together. This page says what we store, why, who else handles it, and how to get it removed.

What we store

WhatWhy
Your account: name, email address and password (stored only in scrambled form)To sign you in and show your name to people in the same events
Events you create or join: details, tasks, schedule, costs, updates and commentsTo run the event workspace and share it with the people in that event, according to their role
Guests a host adds: name and, if the host enters them, email address, WhatsApp number, replies, dietary notes and plus-one namesSo the host can invite guests and keep track of replies
Photos you add to an event galleryTo show them to the people that event's host allows
Feedback you send and automatic error reports (the error text, the screen you were on and your browser type)To fix problems and improve the product

We do not show adverts, sell your information or use advertising trackers. The app keeps your sign-in and a few preferences in your browser's storage so you stay signed in.

If a host added you as a guest

Hosts can add your name and contact details so they can invite you. The host decides what to enter and sends the invitation themselves (by WhatsApp or email). If you want your details changed or removed, ask the host, or contact us and we will remove them.

Who else handles the information

  • Supabase stores the database, sign-in details and photos.
  • Netlify serves the website.
  • Brevo delivers invitation and account emails, when a host chooses to send emails through Gather.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads.
  • WhatsApp: when a host taps “WhatsApp”, their own WhatsApp opens with the message written. Gather does not send WhatsApp messages itself.

These companies may process information outside your country. The event planner works from built-in rules, or from a model that runs inside your own browser where one is available; your event details are not sent to an AI company.

Who can see what

Only people in an event can see it, and what they see depends on the role the host gave them. Guests do not see budgets, internal discussions or organiser-only photos unless the host switches that on. Events set to “Anyone with the link” or “Public” show their name, date, place and description to anyone who has the link.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Deleting an event removes everything in it. Deleting your account (Profile, then “Delete account”) removes your profile, the events you host, your photos and your replies. During the beta we do not keep separate long-term backups.

Your choices

  • See and change your event information in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account yourself from your Profile.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or remove it.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights over your information, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Gather is not meant for children under 13, and accounts should not be created for them.

Contact

Use “Help and feedback” on your Profile in the app. If you do not have an account, reply to the invitation email you received or ask the host who invited you to pass your request on.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
